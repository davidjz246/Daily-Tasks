📲 Add this app to your phone

Once added, it opens with one tap, full-screen, even with no internet.
1
Open this file in Safari (not another browser — this only works in Safari on iPhone).
2
Tap the Share icon at the bottom of the screen (the square with an arrow pointing up ⬆️).
3
Scroll down the menu and tap "Add to Home Screen".
4
Tap "Add" in the top corner. A notepad icon named Tasks will appear on your home screen.
